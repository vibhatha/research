Governance Compliance Scorecard

Alpha Research — AI-Generated Analysis

This scorecard is an alpha-stage research prototype. The scores, grades, and alerts were generated using AI analysis of legislative texts with human advisory input. The data and model have not been independently verified.

  • Do not use these scores to make policy, legal, or administrative conclusions
  • The Lanka Data Foundation (LDF) accepts no responsibility for any decisions or actions taken based on this analysis
  • Methodology and scoring parameters are subject to revision
  • Source data confidence varies (see per-act confidence ratings)

This tool is intended solely for research and exploratory purposes to demonstrate how legislative mandate compliance could be systematically measured.
